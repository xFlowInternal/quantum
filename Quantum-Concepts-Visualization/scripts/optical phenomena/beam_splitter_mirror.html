Beam Splitter Physics

A beam splitter is a partially silvered mirror that divides incident light into two beams traveling at 90° to each other.

Single Photon Mode: Each individual photon goes to EITHER the horizontal OR vertical detector (never both). Over time, the detection ratio approaches the split ratio.

Classical Mode: Continuous light beam is split according to the intensity ratio.

Conservation: All photons are detected - none are lost in the beam splitter.
