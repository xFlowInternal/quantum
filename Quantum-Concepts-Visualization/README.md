# Quantum Optics & Quantum Information Visualizations (HTML Animations)

This repository contains a collection of **interactive HTML-based visual animations** designed to intuitively demonstrate key concepts from **quantum optics** and **quantum information science**.  
The goal of this project is to bridge the gap between **abstract quantum theory** and **visual understanding** using simple, browser-based simulations.

These animations are especially useful for:
- Students learning quantum mechanics or quantum computing
- Educators explaining quantum communication protocols
- Self-learners exploring quantum optics concepts visually

---

## Included Animations

### BB84 Quantum Key Distribution
A visual demonstration of the **BB84 protocol**, illustrating:
- Quantum state preparation
- Basis selection (rectilinear & diagonal)
- Measurement effects
- Eavesdropping detection intuition

---

### Beam Splitter & Mirror
An animation showing how photons behave when interacting with:
- Beam splitters
- Mirrors  
Commonly used in **interferometers** and **quantum optics experiments**.

---

### Optical Qubit Animation
A visualization of an **optical qubit**, typically encoded using:
- Photon polarization
- Superposition states  
Helps in understanding how qubits can be physically realized using light.

---

### Prism & Snell’s Law
A classical-to-quantum bridge animation illustrating:
- Refraction through a prism
- Snell’s Law  
Useful for understanding the optical foundations behind many quantum experiments.

---

### Time-Bin Encoding
A visual explanation of **time-bin qubit encoding**, widely used in:
- Quantum communication
- Fiber-based quantum networks  
Demonstrates early and late photon arrival times representing qubit states.
